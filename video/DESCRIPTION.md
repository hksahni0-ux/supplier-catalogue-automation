# Supplier catalogue automation for FCI London

A 3:02 demo of the round-2 technical assessment. It's an end-to-end Python pipeline that turns
supplier price lists, swatch cards and product pages into FCI's 6 upload CSVs, full-quality
product images and swatch images, plus a report of everything a person should check. Around it:
a Google Drive workspace (price lists in, products in a sheet, results out by date), price-change
comparison, and a React web app with an assistant (NVIDIA Nemotron) whose figures are checked
against the data, and which only runs products after a person presses Confirm.

Every number on screen comes from the real supplier files and the real runs; the app scenes are
screenshots of the working app.
